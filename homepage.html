Good to know

Your questions,
answered clearly.

Start with one meal. Add more of your day when you are ready.

What does TLC show me that my wearable does not?

Your wearable can show sleep and activity. TLC adds your meals and your own check-ins on energy, mood and focus. It brings those signals together in daily insights and a weekly review, so you can explore patterns and decide what to try next.

How does TLC help when I eat out?

Tell TLC your goals, preferences and dietary needs. It suggests dishes from nearby restaurant menus with estimated nutrition. At a participating restaurant, scan the TLC table QR to see suitable choices from that restaurant's menu.

What can I use TLC for at home?

Make a recipe from ingredients you already have, or photograph a finished plate for an estimated nutrition breakdown. TLC Pro adds a personalised week of full recipes, a combined shopping list designed to reduce leftovers, and batch-prep guidance.

What does Luma do?

Luma is your personal coach. Ask by voice or text about food or your day. She uses the goals, meals and check-ins you share, plus wearable data if connected, to suggest a practical next step. As you keep using TLC, your past insights remain part of that context.

How does the zero-waste shopping list work?

With TLC Pro, your weekly meal plan comes with full recipes and one combined shopping list. TLC adds up the ingredients you need across the week, removes duplicates and uses shared ingredients in more than one meal. That helps you buy what you need and use more of it, with fewer leftovers.

How is my data used?

Your data is used to personalise your TLC experience and is not sold to advertisers. You can read how it is handled and how to request deletion in the Privacy Policy.
